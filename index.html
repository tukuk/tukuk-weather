Why the numbers matter

Rain belts and monsoons move twice a year across the tropics, cross-border haze periodically closes schools, and mid-latitude countries plan around fronts, snow and storm surges. Reading the measurements — humidity, pressure, wind speed, AQI — turns a forecast into decisions: when to plant, when to carry an umbrella, when driving is most dangerous.

For the night sky (moon phase & twilight), see Tonight’s Sky in the NASA complex — one family of data, two points of view. Ground shaking? Check the Earthquake Complex for the live USGS list.
